Risk first.
Size second.

Position Sizer answers the only question that matters before entry: how much can I buy without betting more than I meant to lose? One calculator for crypto, equities and E-mini futures — plus a live portfolio heatmap across every broker account.

₿ CRYPTO ◈ EQUITIES ⌗ E-MINI FUTURES LONG / SHORT WORKS OFFLINE
The governing equation
Size = Equity × Risk % | Entry − Stop |

Futures variant: Contracts = (Equity × Risk% × Rate) / (|Entry − Stop| × $/point) — floored to whole contracts, tick-aligned, with actual risk recomputed.

01

Three inputs. Zero guesswork.

Every trade starts from the account, not the chart. Define what a loss is worth, and the position size falls out deterministically.

1

DEFINE THE RISK

Account equity × risk-per-trade. Presets from 0.5% to 5%, in your account currency — USD, AUD, EUR, or stablecoins for crypto accounts.

2

MARK THE EXIT

Entry and stop define risk-per-unit. Direction-aware validation: a long's stop must sit below entry, a short's above. FX auto-converts when account and asset currencies differ.

3

TAKE THE SIZE

Units, notional value, risk per share/contract and R-multiple targets — with potential profit and risk/reward computed the instant a target is set.

02

The calculator

Three asset tabs, each with fully independent state — your crypto account balance and your equities account balance live side by side, never overwriting each other.

Live data

Price on tap

Spot prices from CoinGecko for 27+ mapped coins, Yahoo Finance for tickers & contracts. Futures entries track live, refreshing every 15 seconds until you type your own.

FX aware

Cross-currency

AUD account, USD asset? Live rates via Frankfurter convert the risk budget automatically — stablecoins mapped to USD. Rate stays editable for manual override.
